About Us

Driving India's Growth – One Load at a Time

Alaknanda Transport Company is a trusted leader in the transportation of molasses and liquid bulk cargo across India. With a strong legacy built on integrity, safety, and efficiency, we have been at the forefront of India's agricultural and industrial supply chain, ensuring timely and secure delivery of essential by-products like molasses to distilleries, sugar mills, and allied industries

Established with the vision of creating a streamlined and dependable logistics network, Alaknanda Transport is proud to be a specialist transporter, catering to niche requirements that demand precision, compliance, and care. Our fleet of custom-designed tankers is maintained to the highest standards and operated by trained professionals who understand the value of time-sensitive and quality-assured transport.

Why Choose Alaknanda Transport?

Nationwide Coverage:

Specialized Fleet:

Safety & Compliance First:

Experienced Team:

What we do

Molasses Transportation

Our core service is the bulk transport of molasses—a byproduct of sugar production—used primarily by distilleries and cattle feed industries. We handle full truckload shipments across long distances with safety, speed, and regulatory compliance.

Custom Liquid Bulk Logistics

We offer custom-designed logistics solutions for other compatible liquid materials that require specialized handling and transport methods.

Pan-India Transport Network

From Uttar Pradesh to Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra to Assam—we cover every corner of India. Our network is optimized for fast route planning, real-time tracking, and seamless load management.
